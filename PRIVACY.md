# Privacy

ailove is a self-signed developer preview. It operates on the iPhone where you set it up. You choose the model and voice accounts used by this copy.

- **Screen and actions.** A phone task can read on-screen text and images and send relevant content to the configured model as task context. The app can then tap, type or swipe while processing the task. Review the model provider's privacy terms, especially before using tasks on screens with personal information.
- **Microphone.** Live voice uses microphone access you allow in iOS. During an active voice session, audio is sent to the configured voice service. Use **End** to close the session.
- **Built-in VPN.** The tunnel routes a local connection to services on the same iPhone. The normal internet and DNS routes stay with iOS. Starting the built-in tunnel can switch away from an existing active VPN.
- **Accounts and pairing.** The app stores signing-independent account and pairing state on the iPhone. Use your own accounts and iPhone. Keep pairing files, access tokens and device passcodes private.
- **Optional external connections.** Enable an external connection or relay only when you intend to let that client interact with the phone. Use services you trust.

iOS controls microphone, developer and network permissions. You can review those permissions in Settings and sign out of accounts in the app.
